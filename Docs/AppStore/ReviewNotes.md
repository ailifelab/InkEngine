# App Review Notes（提交前按最终候选核对）

No account, login, subscription or demo credentials are required. This is a local document editor and reader, not a bookstore, content subscription service or public social network. It does not bundle copyrighted novels or download a novel catalog.

1. On first launch choose 新建文稿 (New Document), type or paste the public example below, then save.
2. Switch 编辑 / 阅读 (Edit / Read). Tap Play in the reader toolbar; pause, resume, rate and paragraph navigation are available there.
3. 字号与朗读设置 (Reading & Speech Settings) changes the local voice. List only the voices included in the final submitted build. No manuscript is sent to a cloud TTS provider.
4. 隐私与使用说明 (Privacy & Usage) is accessible from the library and reading settings; on Mac it is also in the app menu. It works offline.
5. 附近设备 (Nearby Devices) is optional. Local Network permission is used for Bonjour discovery and paired encrypted document transfer. Denying permission does not prevent local editing, reading or bundled voice synthesis. Both devices must verify the pairing code before transfer. Remembered peers may be discovered again on startup/foreground.
6. Background audio is used only while reading aloud; pause/stop controls are available. Final build must be checked for interruption, lock screen and low-memory behavior.
