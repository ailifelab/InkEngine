# Usage & Open Source

Ink Engine is for editing, reading and listening to documents you have the right to use. Content remains the property of its rights holders. The app does not provide a novel library or public publishing service. Do not distribute other people's work without authorization. Check the recipient and selected content before nearby sync or system sharing.

Keep backups of important documents. Editing an original file on Mac writes to the file you authorized. Deleting copies, clearing caches, overwriting files and revoking device trust have different effects. Speech may mispronounce uncommon characters, characters with multiple readings and complex text; it does not replace human review.
